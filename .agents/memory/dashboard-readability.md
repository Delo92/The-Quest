---
name: Dashboard readability
description: Contrast baseline for The Quest's authenticated operational dashboards.
---

Authenticated dashboards use a charcoal page surface, clearly lighter cards and form fields, visible borders, brighter secondary text, and readable Poppins typography for operational text and controls. Keep small labels legible, and make every sign-out action a visible labeled button rather than icon-only or plain inline text. The public site's typography and dark visual treatment remain unchanged.

**Why:** The previous near-black page, card, and field surfaces caused eye strain and made operational controls difficult to scan during long sessions. The owner also reported that condensed dashboard text was hard to read and logout was easy to miss.

**How to apply:** Keep dashboard-specific surfaces and typography scoped to authenticated dashboard shells; reserve display fonts for the public site. Use high-contrast, text-labeled sign-out buttons on admin, host, talent, and viewer dashboards.
