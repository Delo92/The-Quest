## Rule
Contestants and hosts each choose their own nonprofit and a rate from 1% through 10%, inclusive, applied only to their eligible gross payout share. The Quest separately sets its 1%–10% rate and platform recipient. Platform matching uses either that platform rate against each participant's contribution or a dollar-for-dollar cash target; no match may exceed the matched person's contribution, and all matches combined stay within The Quest's own nonprofit allocation. Any unused platform allocation goes to the configured platform recipient. Nomination/application acknowledgment and its platform-rate/recipient snapshot are separate from the contestant/host declaration. Prize payouts require a nonprofit name, a valid individual rate, and acknowledgment of that exact rate. Legacy `consentToDonate` does not satisfy that requirement. Disbursements remain manually recorded; the app does not initiate transfers.

**Why:** Individual rates are participant choices, while the platform rate is an administrative setting and its match is a redistribution of the platform's capped allocation, not an extra contribution. A prior generic consent cannot establish acceptance of the current selected rate.

**How to apply:** Keep join/nomination disclosures, profile declarations, payout eligibility, match calculations, admin financial views, and audit records aligned. Require renewed acknowledgment when an individual changes their selected rate; fail closed when the platform rate or recipient is missing.
