## Rule
Contestants and hosts choose their own nonprofit and a contribution rate from 1% through 10% in their own account. If they leave the rate unset, the 10% default applies. Nominators are never asked to choose or guess those details, and missing nonprofit settings never block nomination intake. Before prize payout, the participant must name a nonprofit and acknowledge the effective rate; a rate change requires a new acknowledgment. The Quest matches the same dollar amount as each participant's actual contribution to that participant's selected nonprofit. Combined matches cannot exceed The Quest's available share. There is no separate Quest contribution rate, matching mode, or Quest-selected recipient. Disbursements remain manually recorded; the app does not initiate transfers.

**Why:** The owner clarified that the participant sets their own giving choices, The Quest matches dollars rather than applying a second percentage, and a nominator cannot know what a nominee would choose. Capping aggregate matches at The Quest's available share preserves the existing funding limit without introducing a separate platform rate.

**How to apply:** Keep nomination intake free of nonprofit questions or acknowledgments. Store participant choices only through their own account declaration, apply the 10% default when the rate is absent, and require a recorded acknowledgment snapshot before payout. Calculate and report each Quest match dollar-for-dollar, subject only to the aggregate available-share cap.
