## Rule
Contestants and hosts are required to contribute 1–10% of eligible earnings to a nonprofit they select in their own account; if they leave the rate unset, the 10% default applies. Nomination and host intake may capture an optional nonprofit suggestion or preference, but it is nonbinding and must remain separate from the participant's payout choice. Nominators never set a nominee's nonprofit or rate. Before prize payout, the participant must name a nonprofit and acknowledge the effective rate; a rate change requires a new acknowledgment. The Quest is required to match each participant's actual contribution dollar-for-dollar, without an available-share cap. There is no separate Quest contribution rate, matching mode, or Quest-selected recipient. Disbursements remain manually recorded; the app does not initiate transfers.

**Why:** The owner clarified that participant contributions and the dollar-for-dollar Quest match are both mandatory. Public copy and match calculations must not make the match conditional on available funds. Each participant sets their own nonprofit and rate; a nominator cannot know what a nominee would choose. Optional suggestions can be collected for context without deciding the participant's payout destination. The owner also confirmed that this policy is authoritative when a campaign uses Quest's standard nonprofit program and already supplies the relevant contribution and payout rules.

**How to apply:** Optional intake suggestions may be collected, but store them separately and never preselect or overwrite a participant's account choice. The contribution and dollar-for-dollar match are mandatory; nomination intake may include the required acknowledgment of the policy, but must not require a nonprofit name or rate. Store participant choices through their own account declaration, apply the 10% default when the rate is absent, and require a recorded acknowledgment snapshot before payout. Record each match at the full actual participant contribution; show platform share separately without using it to reduce the match liability. Disbursements remain manual. For campaign requests that use standard Quest policy, apply this contract and align campaign copy to it; do not ask the owner to restate contribution or payout terms unless they explicitly request an exception.
