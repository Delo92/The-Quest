---
name: Contestant profile depth and motion
description: Visual direction for public contestant profile pages.
---

Public contestant profiles should have more visual depth than a flat black background, with restrained lighting tied to the competition accent and scroll-linked 3D movement on media. Honor reduced-motion preferences.

**Why:** The user asked for richer backgrounds and 3D movement during scrolling after reviewing the contestant profile.

**How to apply:** Keep the profile readable and media-first; use subtle ambient layers and perspective shifts rather than constant motion or effects that compete with contestant content.
