name: Host voting POS scope
description: Claim boundaries for describing The Quest's live-event point-of-sale capability.
---

The verified on-site point-of-sale flow is for audience voting: a host can download an event QR code, attendees open the competition in in-person voting mode, available paid vote packages use The Quest checkout, and hosts can review online and in-person vote totals separately. Voter checkout payments are processed by The Quest to add votes; they are not sent directly to a nonprofit at checkout.

**Why:** The host offer should explain the live venue workflow, but current product evidence does not establish general admission, merchandise, concessions, or other event-sale checkout. Voter-facing copy must not imply that a vote payment goes directly to a nonprofit when the existing checkout does not route funds that way.

**How to apply:** Describe this as an on-site voting POS or point of sale for audience voting. Use support or contribution language for paid voting and state that the payment adds votes; do not call it a direct nonprofit donation. Do not claim The Quest handles all event sales unless those separate flows are confirmed.
