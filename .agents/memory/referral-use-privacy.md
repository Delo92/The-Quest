---
name: Referral-use privacy
description: What talent and host code owners may learn from referral activity
---

Talent and host referral-code owners may receive an aggregate code-use count only. Do not expose the identity or contact details of people who use their codes in owner-facing notifications.

**Why:** The user considers sharing a voter's contact with an unrelated artist or host a breach of trust.

**How to apply:** Keep referral-use notices limited to the code and aggregate count. Review any new owner-facing referral analytics for names, emails, phone numbers, or other identifying details.
