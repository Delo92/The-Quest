name: Quest admin navigation
description: Top-level destinations in the admin dashboard sidebar, including the placement of payroll tools.
---

Payroll & Agreements is a top-level destination in the System section of the admin dashboard, alongside Storage, Voting Stats, and Settings. Voting Stats is for vote, referral, and payment tracking; reserve Analytics for the separate site-traffic view under Settings → Diagnostics.

**Why:** Payroll is an operational workflow with its own payees, agreements, batches, and ledger, so hiding it inside platform configuration makes it harder to discover. The voting/referral dashboard was also confused with website analytics and diagnostics.

**How to apply:** Keep desktop and mobile navigation pointed at the outer Payroll & Agreements and Voting Stats destinations. Reserve the Settings sub-tabs for Platform Settings and Diagnostics, with Analytics inside Diagnostics describing site traffic rather than votes.
