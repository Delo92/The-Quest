name: Competition branding scope
description: The boundary between shared competition UI/UX and event-specific branding.
---

Applying Starr Struck's UI/UX to Chronic Cuties or other competitions means sharing layout, spacing, readability, and interaction patterns—not its pink branding. Preserve each competition's configured accent. Events without a custom theme retain the platform orange and neutral background.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected an unintended change that made Raplahoma and other unthemed competitions pink. They requested UI/UX consistency, not a shared event palette.

**How to apply:** Never copy a reference competition's colors into shared defaults or hard-code its tint into shared backgrounds. Keep the transparent title treatment and compact mobile summaries independent of theme colors.
